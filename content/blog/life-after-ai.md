Back in May, I wrote about how [AI is killing the website I live from](/blog/death-by-ai). Today, that post has made the front page of Hacker News. Instead of addressing the [hundreds of comments](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50542705) individually, let me write a quick update.

## How are we doing?

As of November 2026, traffic is still in free fall. I lose a double-digit percentage of my traffic every month. Last Sunday, I had under 1,000 unique visitors for the first time in memoriam.

Financially, I'm still doing okay. My income is much lower than last year, but it has somewhat stabilized. Above all, it still covers my bills. I earn more per visitor than a few years ago, and it partly makes up for the dwindling audience.[^1]

Psychologically, it's a bit harder. My identity is invariably tied to this website. I have poured nearly a decade of my life into this website. It's hard to motivate myself to work for a fifth of the impact. Tomorrow it will be a tenth, and after that who knows?

## Where do we go from here?

At this point, I have gone through all the stages of grief, and I have reached acceptance. I have thought long and hard about my options.

The first option is to give up. Take a sabbatical and ride a motorcycle around the world while the website slowly drifts into irrelevance. When the money runs out, get a job as a software developer. Sure, that is *also* being threatened by AI and all my developer friends tell me that work has become miserable, but at least I won't starve!

The second option is to start something else. Technically, that's what I'm already doing with [A Good Start](https://agoodstart.de). I don't love selling health insurance - in fact it's soul-crushingly boring - but it's honest work, it helps immigrants, and it lets me run All About Berlin on the side.

The third option is to double down. Keep building cool things, ride this thing until the wheels fall off. Sure, All About Berlin won't attract the crowds it used to, but I can please the crap out of those remaining visitors. A smaller user base gives me enough bandwidth to answer reader mail again. It makes a forum or some sort community manageable. We can make it real cosy for those who remain.

Realistically, it will a bit of options two and three. I will probably write about health insurance more than I'd like to, but I will also keep writing guides and building tools for immigrants. Google, OpenAI and Anthropic will keep leeching off of my work and sell it as a subscription, but *it will still help people*, and as long as I can feed myself, I am at peace with that.

I will also focus on what AI can't do: surface information that was not yet written down. I have seen how valuable it was to expose [immigration office wait times](https://allaboutberlin.com/guides/immigration-office/wait-times) and [rent prices](https://allaboutberlin.com/tools/rent-map). I want to create more of that kind of stuff.

I also want to do something a little bolder: [start a forum](/blog/forum). In 2027. Reddit has killed forums long ago, and now AI is killing Reddit. My plan might sound nuts, but we need community and advice from other humans more than ever. I'm not sure if I can actually get that community off the ground, but it's worth a try.

What I will not do is allow All About Berlin to enshittify. There is no better moment to reaffirm my principles than when they are challenged. I will bear the cost. If this website is to fade into oblivion, it will do so with dignity.
