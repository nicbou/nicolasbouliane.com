Sakura pens are great, but they're hard to tell apart from one another, especially in a stuffed pencil case at the bottom of a messenger bag. The pens have faint size markings, but some have worn off after a week.

I have marked mine with coloured tape on which I wrote their size.

![Pens of different sizes](/images/pen-markings.jpg)

Now it's easier to fish the right one out of my pencil case.

![Pens in pencil case](/images/pencil-case.jpg)

I mostly use my plastic nib pen and my brush pen, so I made them a little different.

![Colour-coded pens](/images/pen-marking-special-pens.jpg)

I added an extra tape loop on the brush pen to help me find it by touch.

![Pen marked with tape](/images/pen-marking-texture.jpg)

These little fixes always felt normal to me. However my friends often hint at it being a very "me" thing to do.

It's certainly a *Bouliane* thing to do. It is accepted that every Bouliane family visit starts with the patriarch's tour of all the things he's done *properly*.

As children, it was a quirk that we obliged in our elders, but then it happend to my father, and now it's happening to me. There is not a single engineer among us, but the engineering spirit certainly flows through our veins.

With time, I have come to see the beauty in it. These little fixes are a way of investing in and exercising agency over one's lived environment. The world is a wobbly table, and you have the power to slide a shim under its leg.

## Other fixes

My have different shapes to help me distinguish them by touch.

![Different-shaped keys](/images/key-shape.jpg)

My cables are colour-coded: red for USB-C, yellow for micro USB, blue for lighting. I don't really do it anymore, mostly because everything is USB-C now.

![Colour-coded cables](/images/cable-marking.jpg)

It helps me find tiny, same-shaped adapters inside my luggage.

![Colour-coded adapters](/images/adapter-marking.jpg)

When I rewired my motorcycle, I marked the wiring I added in green, and wrapped one end of SAE cables in blue, to avoid accidentally plugging the wrong ends together. Blue is the side the power comes from.

![Colour-coded SAE connectors](/images/PXL_20220301_114646648.jpg)
